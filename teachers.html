✦ What’s in the Curriculum

Everything Ready.
Nothing Left to Guess.

Every lesson is ready to project in class, with no special preparation or equipment.

📚

Interactive Lessons

Each lesson is a sequence of short screens that leads the class from looking to making.

🖼️

Artworks and Artists

Lessons open with works by artists such as Rothko, Munch, Frida Kahlo, Monet and O’Keeffe, as a starting point for talk and work.

💬

Questions for Looking

Every lesson asks students to look slowly and talk about what they see.

✍️

Studio Work in Steps

Short instructions, step by step, for drawing and for experiments in colour, line and composition.

🪞

Reflection

Each lesson closes by looking at the work and asking students what they discovered.

🌍

Hebrew and English

Most lessons are available in Hebrew and English, with one click to switch.
