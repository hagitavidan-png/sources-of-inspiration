✦ The Curriculum

Seven Units. One Big Question:
Where Does Creativity Come From?

Each unit introduces a different source of inspiration. Students look closely, discuss what they notice, and develop their own artwork.

1
❤️
Unit 01
Personal Experience as a Source of Inspiration

How do artists use feelings as raw material? Students explore how emotion shapes color, line, and composition, from Munch's Scream to their own visual diaries.

6 lessons 45–60 min each
What colour is sadness?
The art of Edvard Munch
Creating an emotional landscape
2
🌿
Unit 02
Nature as Inspiration

From Monet's water lilies to the geometry of a leaf. Students discover how the natural world has inspired artists across all movements and periods.

6 lessons 45–60 min each
Patterns in nature & art
Monet and the Impressionists
Botanical drawing & abstraction
3
🎵
Unit 03
Music & Rhythm in Art

Can you paint a sound? Students bridge the senses, exploring Kandinsky's synesthesia, jazz-inspired abstract art, and how rhythm becomes visual movement.

6 lessons 45–60 min each
Kandinsky & synesthesia
Painting music: jazz & abstract
Rhythm, repetition, movement
4
🧠
Unit 04
Memory & Identity

Students explore how Frida Kahlo, Marc Chagall, and other artists used memories in their work, then create a portrait that expresses something about themselves.

6 lessons 45–60 min each
Frida Kahlo, self as subject
Memory map exercise
Creating an identity portrait
5
🌍
Unit 05
Culture & Symbols

Art as cultural language. Students decode visual symbols from world traditions and create work that honors and reimagines cultural heritage.

6 lessons 45–60 min each
Visual symbols across cultures
Folk art & modern remix
My cultural symbol project
6
🎨
Unit 06
Personal Style Development

The final unit. Students synthesize everything they've learned to develop their own artistic voice and create a capstone portfolio piece.

6 lessons 45–60 min each
Finding your visual language
Artist statement writing
Final portfolio project

✦ Why This Curriculum

Built for the Way Students
Actually Think

Not a collection of lessons. A coherent journey from sensation to self-expression.

🎭

Inquiry-Led Learning

Each lesson begins with a question and time to look closely. The discussion gives students a starting point for their own work.

🖼️

World-Class Art as Anchor

Students encounter works by Van Gogh, Kahlo, Kandinsky, and Klee. They discuss the artists’ choices and try related approaches in their own work.

✍️

Reflection Built In

Written and visual reflection prompts are woven into every lesson. Students develop critical thinking alongside creative skills.

🌐

Artists from Many Places

From Hokusai in Japan and Frida Kahlo in Mexico to Munch in Norway and Rothko in New York.

🧭

Screen by Screen

Each lesson is projected in class one screen at a time, so it is easy to lead and easy to follow.

📱

Every Device, Every Room

Open the curriculum on a smartboard, laptop, or tablet.
