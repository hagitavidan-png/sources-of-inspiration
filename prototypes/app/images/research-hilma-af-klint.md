# Hilma af Klint (1862–1944): image research, kept for a later lesson

Researched for Lesson 2.1 (October 2026), then set aside: the lesson uses Sophie Taeuber-Arp instead. Nothing here is
embedded in the app. Status words as in `images/2-1/SOURCES.md`.

## The artworks

*The Ten Largest* (*De tio största*), 1907, Group IV, Nos. 1–10, tempera on paper mounted on canvas, about 322 × 239 cm
each (Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:The_Ten_Largest_(Hilma_af_Klint)). The artist died on
21 October 1944, so her works are in the public domain where the term is life + 70 years (Sweden, the EU, Israel) from
1 January 2015.

Open point for the United States: under 17 U.S.C. § 303(a), a work created before 1978 and first published between 1978
and 2002 stays protected until at least the end of 2047. Her paintings were first shown in 1986 (LACMA, *The Spiritual
in Art*); whether the catalogue reproductions then count as publication of the paintings has not been checked.

## Rights findings

- **The foundation's position.** The image-rights page (https://hilmaafklint.se/image-rights/) says the Hilma af Klint
  Foundation owns the photographic rights to all images of her works. It licenses files itself, with Moderna Museet's
  picture agency for 1–4 images, and for a charge. Approval is case by case, mainly for publications about the artist,
  and the credit line is "Courtesy of The Hilma af Klint Foundation". Its low-resolution downloads are for private use only.
  (The page could not be opened from the build environment; this is taken from search results.)
- **Swedish law since 1 January 2023 (SFS 2022:1712, implementing Article 14 of the EU DSM Directive).** § 49 a of
  the Copyright Act (1960:729) now ends: "Rätten gäller dock inte om det huvudsakliga motivet för bilden utgörs av ett
  konstverk för vilket upphovsrätt inte längre gäller". In English: the photographic-image right does not apply where the
  main subject of the picture is an artwork no longer protected by copyright. The text is quoted from
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Copyright_rules_by_territory/Sweden. Whether the change also covers
  photographs taken before 2023 is reported as unsettled. The primary texts (riksdagen.se, svenskforfattningssamling.se)
  could not be opened from here.
- **Conclusion.** The visitor photographs (set A) carry the photographer's own public-domain release, so the
  photograph layer is clear. The artwork layer is public domain in life + 70 countries; the US point above is open.
